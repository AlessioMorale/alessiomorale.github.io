What this site stores and sends, as of the last edit of this page.

**Analytics.** The site loads Google Analytics (measurement ID `G-EGSP92JKW9`) only after you press Approve in the banner, and only on the published site. Press Deny, or ignore the banner, and no analytics script is loaded. Your choice is stored for 31 days in a first-party cookie named `cookie-notice-option`.

**Fonts.** Archivo, IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono are served from this site. No font request goes to Google.

**Video.** The Slammer page embeds one YouTube video, served from youtube-nocookie.com. Opening the page sends a request to YouTube.

**Comments, forms, ads.** None. The site has no comment system, login or newsletter form.

**Search.** The search page opens a Google search restricted to this site in a new tab. Google handles that request under its own policy.

**Contact.** Questions about this page: use the links on the [about page](/info/).
